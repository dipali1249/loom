The Loom Room

One cloth. Ninety seconds. The rule will change — your job is to keep up. Tap what the rule asks for, and only that.

The Rule
A banner tells you what to tap. It mutates every 15 seconds, without warning.
Inhibition
Tapping something the rule does not want is an error. So is missing what it does.
The Trace
At the end, you see how you behaved around every rule change — not just your score.

Shapes drift in and fade fast. Tap a green circle when the rule says green or circle; tap a red triangle when it says red or triangle. Wrong taps and misses both count against you.
